ExoSett component

Pod

A pod is a volumetric component designed for installation within one bay of an ExoSett accommodation frame.

It remains a pod whether it is in manufacture, in transit, installed in a building or being refurbished for reuse. It may contain a compact home, a room or another usable space. When installed, each pod is supported by the accommodation frame, rather than by neighbouring pods. The associated service frame provides access and shared utilities.

One pod, or several

One pod can be a home, but one home can also comprise several pods. Pods can connect horizontally or vertically to create larger dwellings and other spaces. Connecting pods into a dwelling does not erase their identity as separate physical components: each still occupies its own bay.

Pod size and dwelling size

Pod size is fixed within a building class, but may differ between building classes. Dwelling sizes can vary by combining different numbers of pods. Compatible pods share the dimensional and connection requirements of their building class.

ExoSett does not prescribe a universal pod size. The ISO 668 1CCC envelope used in the current modelling is an illustrative reference, not a size limit for the architecture.

Made for use and reuse

A pod can be manufactured and fitted out away from the building, then transported to site for installation. Later, it can be removed for refurbishment and reuse.

The accommodation frame carries the primary structural loads, so pods do not provide the principal support for those beside or above them. Removal still requires planned disconnection of services, access, restraints and any connections to other pods.
