Research and Reading

ExoSett has developed within a much wider discussion about modular construction, adaptable buildings, circular construction and industrialised building.

This section collects a small, curated selection of research and other published thinking that helps inform, test or challenge the ideas behind ExoSett. Each entry considers why the source is interesting and where ExoSett may take a different view.
