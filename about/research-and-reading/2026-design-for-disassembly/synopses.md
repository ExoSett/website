## Synopsis

A research-by-design comparison of original apartment-block plans and a DfD redesign examines component reuse potential.

## Main arguments and findings

Separation and standardisation improve design indicators. Table 2 estimates reusable mass shares of 31% and 67%.

## Qualifications and limitations

Single design case; recovery was not demonstrated. Reversible joints and economics require further investigation.

## Relevance to ExoSett

ExoSett interpretation: separation of supporting frames, services and accommodation could support module exchange during use. This paper does not validate ExoSett.
