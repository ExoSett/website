About ExoSett

The ExoSett Value Proposition

A building system for the long term

ExoSett proposes a way to create buildings that can adapt to changing needs while retaining the value of their components. Rather than treating every building as a distinct project, it provides an architecture for creating families of buildings that share a common specification.

An ExoSett building system brings together a common building class specification, compatible components and the means to demonstrate their conformance. It can support many individual buildings, each with its own configuration and purpose, evolving over time while remaining part of the same long-lived system.

Clearly defined specifications make this possible. They provide a consistent basis for design, distributed manufacturing and testing for conformance, with the aim of making components from different suppliers interchangeable. This capability has not yet been demonstrated across independent manufacturers.

The result is an architecture in which individual buildings can change without abandoning the common system on which they are based.

Buildings that genuinely evolve

An ExoSett building is designed around accommodation frames, service frames and independently replaceable pods.

The accommodation frame provides structural support, while the service frame provides access and utilities. Pods are intended to be installed, removed, replaced or relocated without dismantling the surrounding building.

This is intended to allow buildings to be reconfigured as requirements change. Accommodation can be expanded, reduced, rearranged or repurposed, while preserving much of the investment in existing components.

Importantly, the nature of the accommodation itself can evolve. New pod designs, technologies and uses can be introduced without necessarily changing the underlying system or sacrificing compatibility with existing components.

Standardise the system, not the buildings.

Preserving long-term value

Buildings represent substantial investments in materials, manufacturing, labour and infrastructure. Yet changes in requirements can make existing buildings expensive to adapt or unsuitable for their original purpose.

ExoSett aims to preserve that investment. Components can be maintained, refurbished, recovered and reused, potentially serving several buildings and purposes over their lifetimes.

The useful life of a building component need not end when the building it originally served is changed or dismantled.

Making better use of resources

Reusing complete manufactured components preserves more than their raw materials. It can also preserve the manufacturing effort and embodied energy invested in them.

By making recovery and reuse practical, ExoSett has the potential to reduce demand for new materials, embodied carbon emissions and construction waste.

These environmental benefits are an intended consequence of the architecture, although their extent will need to be established through real-world use and lifecycle assessment.

Greater benefits at scale

The advantages of a common building system can grow as it is adopted across more buildings and over longer periods.

National social housing programmes, prison estates and hotel chains are examples of applications where demand may persist for generations, even as the quantity, location and nature of the accommodation required changes.

A common specification can support multiple manufacturers, established supply chains, repeatable testing and a growing inventory of interchangeable components.

Individual buildings can evolve, components can move between buildings, and new generations of accommodation can be introduced without repeatedly starting from scratch.

A different approach to investment

ExoSett does not promise the lowest initial construction cost. Designing for durability, reconfigurability and reuse may involve additional upfront investment.

Instead, it offers the prospect of better value over the lifetime of a building system, potentially spanning many buildings, configurations and generations of components.

The ambition is to create building systems whose value endures, even as the buildings within them continually evolve.
