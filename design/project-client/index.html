ExoSett project role

Project client

Defining why the building should exist and establishing the authority and financial basis to make it happen.

The Project client originates the proposal for an ExoSett building. They define what the project needs to achieve, provide its financial basis and commission the work needed to determine whether it is viable.

What they bring

The knowledge behind the proposal

A Project client brings:

  • a clear value proposition covering time and cost;
  • a clear functional goal for the building;
  • a funding plan covering provision and operation;
  • a site;
  • a delivery timeframe; and
  • a building lifecycle plan.

The value proposition provides the basis for judging cost, time and emerging design choices.

Responsibilities

Test the proposal before expanding the project

The Project client is responsible for:

  • engaging a Building designer;
  • working with the Building designer to determine whether the proposal is viable;
  • maintaining the financial basis for the project;
  • making the strategic decisions needed for progress; and
  • establishing a Site office once the project is sufficiently viable.

Initial proposal→viability exercise→decision to proceed→establish Site office

What they deliver

Creating the first building specification

The Project client launches the building specification with the help of the Site office. The Site office provides the technical support needed to establish the version-controlled document using the current specification format and schema.

The Project client and Building designer then populate its initial version with an agreed expression of:

  • the functional goal;
  • the site;
  • the delivery timeframe;
  • the value proposition and financial constraints;
  • the funding plan;
  • the lifecycle intent; and
  • strategic approvals and decisions.

Together, they give the first concrete form to that intent. Early work may establish:

  • accommodation-module dimensions;
  • the number and arrangement of accommodation cells;
  • the required mix of accommodation units;
  • the module combinations needed to create them;
  • the broad foundation approach; and
  • initial ideas for roofing and facades.

The client defines the required outcomes, constraints and accommodation needs. Specialist designers and engineers determine the technical means of achieving them. The Site office supports the document and versioning process; responsibility for the information remains with the roles that contribute and approve it. The client and Building designer can use the ExoSett Sketch app to explore early ideas about scale, frame arrangement and capacity.

Cost is considered from the outset. Early building ideas are compared with indicative costs and the client’s value proposition, allowing scope and design to be revised while the project is still fluid.

Shared project specification

Working with the building specification

The ExoSett building specification is a living document. Its early versions capture the project intent; as the project develops, it evolves to capture the detail needed to realise that intent.

The Project client initiates its strategic requirements. Specialist project participants contribute and approve the portions for which they are responsible and appropriately qualified. The Site office ultimately acts upon the approved specification.

The ExoSett modelling repository is the authoritative technical source for its format, schema, examples and versioning guidance.
