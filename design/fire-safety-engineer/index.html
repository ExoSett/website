ExoSett project role

Fire safety engineer

Keeping everyone safe.

The fire safety engineer oversees fire safety across the complete project. The strategy belongs to the building and its changing configurations, not to any single frame, module, service or other component.

Contribution

Develop a building-wide strategy

The role addresses ignition, detection, warning, fire and smoke spread, escape, structural stability, firefighting, inspection and recovery. It establishes the performance required from the building’s components and from the interfaces between them.

The existing Fire safety page sets out the principles and possible measures that inform this work.
