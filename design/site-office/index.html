ExoSett project role

Site office

Guardians of the project life cycle.

The Site office is the cradle-to-grave function that turns the design into a working building, supports its operation and change, and ultimately coordinates its decommissioning. It may be delivered by a team whose membership evolves with the project.

Contribution

Carry knowledge through the whole life cycle

During implementation, the Site office coordinates construction, logistics, sequencing, services, testing and handover. During operation, it maintains the authoritative records needed to inspect, maintain, repair and safely change the building, including installing, removing or refurbishing modules.

At the end of the building’s useful life, the function plans safe decommissioning, disassembly and the appropriate reuse, recovery or disposal of its parts.

Interfaces

Protect the design intent as circumstances change

The Site office is the continuing interface between the project team, contractors, operators, maintainers and building users. It controls records and change so that structural, service, fire-safety and site constraints remain understood throughout the building’s life.

Clear ownership of each decision, configuration and handover allows specialists to contribute at the right time without losing the history on which safe future decisions depend.
