ExoSett project role

Safety engineer

Keeping everyone safe.

The safety engineer oversees safety across the complete project. Fire safety remains an important responsibility within a broader strategy that belongs to the building and its changing configurations rather than to any single component.

Contribution

Develop a building-wide strategy

The role identifies hazards, coordinates risk controls and establishes the performance required from the building’s components and their interfaces. Its fire-safety responsibilities include ignition, detection, warning, fire and smoke spread, escape, structural stability, firefighting, inspection and recovery.

The existing Fire safety page sets out principles and possible measures that inform this part of the work.

Interfaces

Keep every configuration safe

The safety engineer coordinates with the building, module, frame and services specialists and with the site office. The agreed strategy must remain effective through installation, occupation, maintenance, vacant cells, module replacement, recovery and later changes.
