ExoSett project role

Services designer

Coordinating building services through every configuration.

The Services designer coordinates building services and their interfaces with the service frame, accommodation modules and wider site infrastructure.

Contribution

Plan services for use and change

The role defines utilities, capacity, connection points, isolation and maintenance access. It also considers how services can be inspected, adapted and safely changed over the life of the building.

Interfaces

Coordinate routes and connections

The Services designer works from the building-level requirements coordinated by the Building designer. They coordinate the physical delivery of services through the building with the Frame designer, and agree the module connection interface with the Module designer.

That shared interface must define physical locations, connection arrangements, accessibility, isolation and the requirements for safely making and breaking connections when modules are installed or removed. Each specialist is responsible for their side of the interface, but both sides must be agreed as one compatible connection.

The Services designer also works with the Safety officer and Site office so that services can be commissioned, inspected, maintained and safely changed through every permitted building configuration.
