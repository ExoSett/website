ExoSett project role

Foundation / plinth engineer

Designing the vital base of the building.

The foundation or plinth engineer designs the building base and the interface between the ExoSett structure and its particular site.

Contribution

Join a repeatable system to a unique place

The role responds to ground conditions, loads, stability, levels, drainage, durability, access, utilities and the project’s environmental and regulatory context. The base must give the frame a precise and dependable support while accommodating site-specific realities.

Interfaces

Define the line between site and structure

The engineer coordinates closely with the building designer and frame engineer to define geometry, tolerances, reactions, fixings, service entries and construction sequence. The interface should also support inspection, repair and the project’s intended end-of-life strategy.
