Emerging ExoSett project role

Roof designer

Defining the building’s upper enclosure.

The Roof designer is responsible for the roof design and for coordinating its interfaces with the ExoSett structure and the wider building. This role is still being developed.

Provisional scope

Coordinate the roof as part of the building

The role is expected to address structural support, restraint and movement alongside weathering, drainage, insulation, services, safe access, inspection, maintenance and construction. Its detailed responsibilities and deliverables have not yet been fully defined.

The Roof designer will coordinate the roof-to-frame interface with the Frame designer and work with the Building designer, Services designer and Safety officer on requirements that cross their respective design interests.
