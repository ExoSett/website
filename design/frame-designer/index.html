ExoSett project role

Frame designer

The art of specifying the frames that will bear the loads and carry the services.

The frame designer specifies the accommodation and service frames for the project. Together they support the building, receive exchangeable modules and provide organised routes for services.

Contribution

Turn a system into a project structure

The designer determines the frame arrangement, members, connections, stability and load paths for the project’s geometry, use, environment and life cycle. The design must account for occupied, vacant and changing cell configurations rather than treating the frame as an isolated object.

Interfaces

Coordinate loads, services and change

The role defines structural and dimensional interfaces with modules, service routes, façades, access systems and the foundation or plinth. It works with the safety officer on structural performance and with the site office on installation, inspection, maintenance and reconfiguration.
