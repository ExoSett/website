First build your team

An ExoSett modular building project brings together client, design, engineering and coordination roles with clearly defined responsibilities.

The ExoSett system separates design interests so specialists can develop the building’s structure, accommodation and services while coordinating the interfaces between them. Their work comes together in a shared building specification.

Explore early building arrangements in the ExoSett Sketch app, then use the role guides below to understand the responsibilities your project team needs to cover.

ExoSett project roles

Project client representative

Representing the client’s objectives and coordinating decisions within delegated authority.

Building designer

Turning the client’s goals into a coordinated building specification.

Module designer

Defining accommodation modules for manufacture, transport, installation, occupation and refurbishment.

Frame designer

Specifying the structural accommodation and service frames, including their loads, connections and interfaces.

Safety officer

Coordinating safety assurance, regulatory approvals and supporting evidence throughout the building’s life.

Services designer

Bringing services from the site, through the service frames, to defined interfaces for accommodation modules.

Foundation designer

Designing foundations or defining the interface with a podium or other supporting structure.

Roof designer

Developing the roof specification, including support, weatherproofing, drainage and future building changes.

Site office

Administering the building specification, procurement and project records through delivery, operation and later change.

Design guidance for modular accommodation

Accommodation design

Design for occupants, dwelling layouts and accommodation quality within the building’s module and interface constraints.

Choosing a location

Consider access to transport, services and community alongside the site’s suitability for the intended accommodation.

Fire safety

Explore building-wide fire-safety considerations across modules, frames, services and access routes as part of a project-specific strategy.
